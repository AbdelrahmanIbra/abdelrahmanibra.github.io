Professional Identity

B.1

A dual-award degree in Mechatronic Engineering is being completed at Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation, delivered jointly with De Montfort University. The subject was chosen for where its problems sit, at the meeting point of mechanical design, electronics, control and software. Studying under Malaysian and British conventions at once, as an Egyptian international student, has shown that engineering rarely has one correct convention, only ones appropriate to a context.

Every project above turned on the same discipline: getting a specification to survive contact with real hardware. That is also where the engineering judgement shows. The quadruped's cascade architecture was chosen against the obvious contemporary option, because a false positive costs a rescue team the one resource it cannot recover.

Professional and ethical awareness was learned on placement rather than in a lecture. Isolation and lock-off before rewiring is not a formality, and recording what was actually inspected rather than what was presumed satisfactory is the difference between a maintenance record and a fiction.

The final year project raised a different class of question: thermal and visual sensing captures data about people frequently unable to consent, so retention, access and duration had to be reasoned through, making the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 a design constraint. Detection results are reported in graded confidence tiers rather than as a binary verdict, since a system concealing its uncertainty transfers risk to the responder who trusts it.

Diversity, Inclusion & Emerging Technology

Working across Egyptian and Malaysian sites has made diversity practical rather than declarative. The useful skill proved to be translation rather than tolerance, since assumptions about hierarchy, disagreement and the meaning of silence are not shared.

On the shop floor during the Delta migration, a technician of two decades' experience raised objections encoding failure modes recorded in no drawing supplied. His experience was evidence, not resistance.

Inclusion enters the technical work directly. An interface legible only to its designer fails an exhausted first responder, and a detector trained on unrepresentative data may perform unevenly across body types, postures and skin tones. It is a question of whose life the system is reliable for.

Engagement with emerging technology has been substantial: machine vision under resource constraints, ROS-based autonomy with SLAM and path planning, simulation as a form of digital twin, and Industry 4.0 practice observed in smart metering and EV charger manufacture. Most was acquired outside any syllabus, yet treated critically rather than enthusiastically, since an unverified simulation is a confident guess.

Threats

The pace of change in robotics, machine vision and automation is the most obvious threat, since a skill set built narrowly around specific tools will age badly. Over-dependence on software abstraction is closely related, as reliance on a library without the underlying theory removes the ability to diagnose unexpected behaviour. A more serious threat is regulatory and ethical: autonomous systems that make judgements about people are moving towards formal regulation, and algorithmic bias, data protection and explainability are becoming legal obligations rather than optional considerations. Where a system performs unevenly across populations, rescue attention is allocated unequally, and neither good intent nor a high average accuracy constitutes a defence. Each threat argues for the response described by PLO11.

01 · Final Year Project, Phase 2

Vision-Based Robot for Autonomous Search and Survivor Detection in Disaster or Post-Conflict Environments

Supervisor: Ir. Ts. Dr. Alexander Chee Hon Cheong · School of Engineering, APU

The architecture was a product of critical appraisal rather than technology selection. An end-to-end neural detector was the obvious contemporary choice, but its false-positive rate would have wasted responder time precisely where time is scarce. So what reached the model was constrained, instead of a further model being added.

A thermal gate admits only regions with a plausible human heat signature. Classical HOG+SVM part detectors then propose candidates, and a neural stage confirms them. Evaluation compares all three configurations as an ablation, under repeatable simulation conditions with recorded metrics.

02 · Group Design Project

AI-Assisted Wearable Neurorehabilitation System with Stress Monitoring

Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Ts. Dr Sathish Kumar Selva Perumal · Group 4, five engineers · May 2026

The group built a pneumatically actuated glove that assists finger movement for patients recovering from stroke, traumatic brain injury and Parkinson's disease. My subsystem was the stress monitoring module: the part that answers whether the patient wearing it is calm or under strain, so a therapy session can respond to the person rather than only to the schedule.

Two signals are read continuously during use. A MAX30102 optical sensor on the wrist gives heart rate over I2C, and an analog GSR sensor across the fingers gives skin conductance. Both are cleaned on the ESP32, streamed over serial, and labelled by a Python logger into a training set.

Raw physiological data is hostile. The optical sensor returns zero or nonsense whenever the hand lifts off the window, and hand movement throws sudden false spikes into the beat interval. An infrared threshold now discards readings below the minimum valid signal level, and beat-interval validation, exponential smoothing, a Kalman filter and a rate limiter run in series so no impossible jump reaches the feature set. The GSR channel drifts with skin moisture and electrode pressure, so it is smoothed before normalisation.

What the result does not prove. The training set came from one user in one controlled session, so the learned boundary reflects individual physiology and may not transfer to another wearer. The relaxed and stressed labels were assigned from the testing condition rather than validated against a clinical measure, which caps how much the ground truth is worth.

Stating that plainly matters more than the accuracy figure. The module is a monitoring and feedback tool, not a diagnostic device: its output is meant to inform a therapist, never to replace one. All processing runs locally on the ESP32 and host, no personally identifiable data is transmitted, and the optical and electrodermal sensing was chosen to work across skin tones and hand sizes without adjustment.
